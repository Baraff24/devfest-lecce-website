## DevFest Lecce 2026 is coming on October 17

DevFest Lecce 2026 is confirmed: on October 17, the tech community will gather in Lecce for a full day of talks, workshops, and networking organized by GDG Lecce.

The program will focus on Artificial Intelligence, Web & Mobile Development, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and Soft Skills. Sessions can be delivered in Italian or English.

Follow GDG Lecce for updates on the schedule, speakers, and registration.
